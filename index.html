How the spacing check works

The model assumes 60-inch rounds, 6 × 2.5-ft banquet tables, and a 2-ft occupied-chair allowance where chairs are actually placed. The guest rectangles seat people only on their long sides, leaving the ends free. It aims for a 3-ft gap between occupied chair spaces; it highlights clashes and gaps under that target. Table-leg positions, pulled-out chairs, serving routes, door swings and local requirements need an on-site check. An automated check of these candidate placements is not proof that no other arrangement could fit.
